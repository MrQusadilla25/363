1. Introduction

GWStudios (“GWStudios,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) develops and operates Waffen Tag.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Waffen Tag and its service providers may collect or process, why that information is used, when it may be shared, how it may be retained, and how players may request access to or deletion of their information.

This Privacy Policy applies to Waffen Tag distributed through Meta Horizon and Meta Quest and to other services that link to this Privacy Policy.

The exact information processed may vary depending on the features a player uses and changes made to the game.

3. Player Accounts and Authentication

Waffen Tag uses platform and game-service authentication to provide player accounts and online functionality.

The game uses Meta/Oculus authentication and may use Microsoft PlayFab to create, authenticate, access, or maintain game accounts.

Information we may process

  • Meta/Oculus platform user identifiers
  • PlayFab Player ID
  • Photon User ID
  • Player display name or username
  • Account creation and last-use information
  • Authentication information
  • Platform entitlement information
  • Account status
  • Suspension or ban status

These identifiers may be used to authenticate players, associate game information with the correct player, provide multiplayer functionality, preserve game data, prevent abuse, and enforce restrictions.

4. Display Names and Player Profiles

Waffen Tag uses player display names or usernames.

We may process

  • Current display name or username
  • Previous or moderated names where reasonably necessary
  • Player appearance
  • Owned or equipped cosmetics
  • Multiplayer profile information
  • Reports associated with a player
  • Moderation and enforcement information

A player's display name, appearance, cosmetics, voice communication, and gameplay actions may be visible to other players during multiplayer gameplay.

Players should not include sensitive personal information in their display names, voice communications, reports, or other multiplayer interactions.

We may reject, remove, or change names that violate our rules, platform requirements, or moderation standards.

5. Gameplay, Cosmetics, and Game Data

We may process information associated with a player's game account, including:

  • Player progress
  • Gameplay statistics
  • Achievements, where applicable
  • Owned cosmetics
  • Equipped cosmetics
  • Unlocks
  • Player settings
  • Multiplayer room and session activity
  • Reports
  • Moderation history
  • Suspensions and bans

Current monetization

Waffen Tag currently includes in-game cosmetics. As of the effective date of this Privacy Policy, Waffen Tag does not offer items that players can purchase using real-world money.

If real-money purchases or other monetization features are introduced in the future, we may update this Privacy Policy and provide additional disclosures where appropriate.

6. Meta Horizon and Meta Quest Data

Waffen Tag operates on Meta Horizon and Meta Quest and uses Meta/Oculus platform functionality.

Depending on the Meta features and permissions used by the game, we may receive or process information such as:

  • Meta/Oculus platform user identifiers
  • Authentication information
  • Entitlement information
  • Meta age-group classification, where the approved feature is enabled
  • Display names or other profile information made available through approved platform features
  • Device or application information
  • Device-ban or device-integrity status provided by Meta
  • Information necessary to operate requested Meta platform features

We use Meta Horizon User Data and applicable Device User Data only as reasonably necessary to operate, authenticate, secure, support, and provide Waffen Tag and its platform features.

Where enabled, age-group information is used to provide age-appropriate access and meet platform or legal requirements. Device-ban or integrity status is used to prevent unauthorized access, enforce restrictions, and reduce ban evasion. Waffen Tag does not use this status as a general-purpose hardware identifier.

Meta may separately process information under Meta's own terms and privacy policies.

7. Multiplayer and Network Information

Waffen Tag uses Photon Engine for multiplayer and networking functionality.

When players use multiplayer features, GWStudios and its service providers may process information including:

  • IP address
  • Approximate geographic information inferred from an IP address
  • Photon User ID
  • Other player identifiers
  • Connection and disconnection information
  • Photon server region
  • Multiplayer room or lobby membership
  • Matchmaking information
  • Network latency
  • Synchronization information
  • Network errors
  • Multiplayer events required to operate gameplay
  • Information used to detect abusive, duplicate, or unauthorized sessions

We do not intentionally use these systems to obtain a player's precise GPS location.

8. Voice Chat and Microphone Information

Waffen Tag provides multiplayer voice communication using Photon Voice.

When a player uses voice chat, Waffen Tag may request permission to access the device's microphone.

Live voice data is transmitted through Photon Voice as necessary to allow players to communicate with each other.

Voice recordings

GWStudios does not intentionally permanently record or store ordinary live voice-chat audio unless:

  • A moderation or reporting feature requiring such processing is introduced and appropriately disclosed;
  • Processing or retention is required or permitted by applicable law; or
  • A player voluntarily provides a recording to GWStudios for support, moderation, or another legitimate purpose.

Other players may independently record gameplay or voice communications using their own devices or software. GWStudios cannot control recordings independently made by other players.

Players should not disclose sensitive personal information through voice chat.

  • Device or headset type
  • Operating-system information
  • Application version
  • Build number

  • Graphics and rendering information
  • Performance information

10. Security, Anti-Cheat, Reports, and Moderation

Waffen Tag uses moderation and anti-abuse systems to help protect players and game services.

We may process:

  • Player identifiers
  • Application version
  • Authentication and entitlement information
  • Indicators of tampering or unauthorized modification
  • Suspicious gameplay behavior
  • Suspicious networking behavior
  • Cheating or exploit indicators
  • Invalid account-access attempts
  • Ban-evasion indicators
  • Player reports
  • Moderation information
  • Enforcement actions
  • Suspensions and bans
  • Security and abuse-prevention logs

Automated systems may identify suspicious activity. Enforcement decisions may also involve human review when appropriate.

Information may be retained when reasonably necessary to investigate misconduct, enforce restrictions, prevent ban evasion, protect players, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable legal and platform requirements.

Our anti-cheat and security systems are not intended to access unrelated personal files, photographs, contacts, private messages, or call history.

11. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

During normal gameplay, Waffen Tag is not intended to collect:

  • Contact lists
  • SMS or private text messages
  • Telephone call history
  • Precise GPS location
  • Government identification numbers
  • Full payment-card information
  • Health information
  • Browsing history
  • Files unrelated to Waffen Tag
  • Lists of unrelated applications installed on the device

If a future Waffen Tag feature requires additional information or sensitive device permissions, we may update this Privacy Policy and provide additional disclosure or consent when required.

17. Account and Data Deletion

Waffen Tag does not currently provide an in-game account-deletion control.

Players may nevertheless request deletion of personal information associated with their Waffen Tag account by contacting:

ikertyph@gmail.com

Use the subject: Waffen Tag Account Deletion Request

What to include

  • Your Waffen Tag display name
  • Your PlayFab Player ID, if available
  • Your Meta/Oculus username or other information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant game account

Do not send your password, authentication tokens, payment information, government identification, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

17.1 Verification

We may take reasonable steps to verify that the person requesting deletion controls the relevant account.

This is intended to prevent another person from maliciously deleting a player's account.

17.2 Information That May Be Deleted

Subject to reasonable verification and lawful retention requirements, deletion may include information under our control such as:

  • PlayFab account information
  • Player identifiers
  • Display name
  • Player progress
  • Cosmetics and unlocks
  • Player settings
  • Statistics
  • Account-linked gameplay information
  • Account-linked moderation information that is not required to be retained
  • Other personal information associated with the account under our control

17.3 Information That May Be Retained

We may retain limited information where reasonably necessary and permitted by law for:

  • Security
  • Fraud or abuse prevention
  • Enforcement of active bans
  • Prevention of ban evasion
  • Resolving disputes
  • Compliance with applicable law
  • Compliance with platform requirements
  • Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims
  • Maintaining a limited record that a deletion request was completed

Where practical, retained information will be limited and restricted from ordinary use.

Meta, Microsoft, Photon, Vercel, Discord, and other platform or infrastructure providers may independently retain certain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

17.4 Effect of Account Deletion

Account deletion may be permanent.

After deletion:

  • Player progress may not be recoverable
  • Cosmetics and unlocks may be lost
  • Settings may be removed
  • Previous account information may no longer be available
  • Launching Waffen Tag again may result in a new account or player record
  • Lawfully retained security or ban information may continue to apply
